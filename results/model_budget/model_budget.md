# Parameter counts and training budgets (I-5 methods)

Generated by `scripts/report/model_budget.py` from `results/model_budget/param_counts.json` and the run records listed in `results/model_budget/model_budget.json`. Descriptive (`used_for_decisions` false). Embed + extract = the networks used at test time (encoder and decoder; WMNet frozen encoder transforms included); discriminators are used in training only. All times are from one NVIDIA A40 per run; WMNet hours are the sum over seeds 0-3.

Time basis (not identical across methods; code lines in `results/model_budget/source_excerpts.md`):

- WMNet: sum over epochs of train.epochs[].seconds (src/trackd_wm/train.py): from the start of the epoch's training loop to the CUDA synchronisation after its last step; excludes checkpoint writing, process start-up and all evaluation (WMNet training runs no in-loop validation).
- MBRS and HiDDeN: phase 1 (pilot G3): sum of g3.json epoch_seconds (src/trackd_wm/pilot.py epoch_seconds), the wall time from the run-start marker to the last checkpoint, which includes the baseline's own per-epoch validation and checkpoint writing; phase 2 (I-5 continuation): train.wall_seconds (src/trackd_wm/i5.py), the wall time of the baseline's training subprocess, which additionally includes interpreter start-up and loading of the epoch-20 checkpoint; the I-5 test-split evaluation starts after it and is excluded from both phases.
- Consequence: baseline hours include the baseline's own per-epoch validation and checkpoint writing, which WMNet hours exclude; the share of validation in the baseline hours is not separated in the records. Process overhead outside the epochs is small where it can be checked: in phase 1 the subprocess wall time exceeds the summed epoch times by 0.9 s (MBRS) and 1.4 s (HiDDeN). Phase 2 runs at 273.2 s per epoch (MBRS) and 65.1 s (HiDDeN), against 280.9 s and 66.6 s in phase 1.

## Reading

- Each baseline is one trained model (seed 0) with a different training length (120 epochs against 20) and, for MBRS, a parameter count about two orders of magnitude larger; comparisons in `results/i5_compare/` are therefore conditional on these budgets and do not isolate the architecture.
- Training compute per trained model: MBRS 9.15 GPU-h and HiDDeN 2.18 GPU-h, against a mean of 0.67 (V2), 0.61 (V3) and 0.54 (V1+) GPU-h per WMNet seed. The time bases differ (above): the baseline hours include the baselines' own per-epoch validation and checkpoint writing, so the ratio of training compute is an upper bound for the baselines relative to WMNet.
- Parameter ratio, denominator stated: MBRS embed + extract 20,805,391 divided by the embed + extract count of V2 (283,998, fixed encoder transform included) is 73.26; divided by the trainable count of V2 (262,806) it is 79.17, and divided by V1+ (262,892, all trainable) 79.14. The existing label "about 74 times the parameters of WMNet" states no denominator; it rounds the first ratio (73.26) upwards. Replacement wording is proposed in `reports/analysis-job104.md` as a flagged numerical change and is not applied to any reported text.
- The MBRS continuation departs from an uninterrupted 120-epoch run (fresh discriminator and optimiser at epoch 20); its effect on the final model is not measured.
